Staten Island is the southern most [[burrow]] of [[New York]], it is also the least developed. 

# The North Side

The north of the island is more developed and densely populated than the south. It is where all of the major markets, entertainment and business centers are located. People live in low rise buildings organized into blocks. 
The most important structure in the whole Island is also located here, it is the [[Staten Island Ferry]] 

# The South Side
Apple orchards fill the south side of the island. It is the food production hub of [[Staten Island]]
